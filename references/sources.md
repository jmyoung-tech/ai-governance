# AI Governance Sources

Curated list of trusted sources for AI governance news, regulation, and analysis. Edit freely to add, remove, or reorganize.

Last updated: 2026-05-21

## Regulators & government bodies

### European Union
- [EU AI Office](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-office) — Commission body running AI Act implementation and enforcement
- [AI Act Service Desk](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en) — official Commission helpdesk for AI Act implementation questions
- [European Commission AI page](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/artificial-intelligence) — official EU AI policy hub
- [European AI Board](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/ai-board) — Member State coordination body
- [European Data Protection Board (EDPB)](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/) — guidance on GDPR/AI intersection

### United States — federal
- [ai.gov](https://www.ai.gov/) — federal AI policy hub
- [America's AI Action Plan](https://www.ai.gov/action-plan) — federal AI strategy issued under EO 14179
- [EO: Removing Barriers to American Leadership in AI (Jan 2025)](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/removing-barriers-to-american-leadership-in-artificial-intelligence/) — revokes prior AI executive orders, directs the AI Action Plan
- [EO: Eliminating State Law Obstruction of National AI Policy (Dec 2025)](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/12/eliminating-state-law-obstruction-of-national-artificial-intelligence-policy/) — directs federal action regarding state AI laws
- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) — AI RMF 1.0 + GenAI profile + crosswalks
- [US AI Safety Institute (US AISI)](https://www.nist.gov/aisi) — frontier-model evaluations and safety guidance
- [US Copyright Office AI initiative](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/) — guidance on AI-generated works
- [FTC AI enforcement](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog?topic=artificial+intelligence) — consumer-protection cases involving AI claims
- [EEOC AI guidance](https://www.eeoc.gov/ai) — AI in employment decisions

### United States — states
- [Colorado AI Act resources (Colorado AG)](https://coag.gov/) — search "SB 24-205" for guidance
- [California Privacy Protection Agency (CPPA)](https://cppa.ca.gov/) — automated decisionmaking technology rules
- [NYC DCWP — Local Law 144 (AEDT)](https://www.nyc.gov/site/dca/about/automated-employment-decision-tools.page)
- [Multistate.ai legislative tracker](https://www.multistate.ai/) — US state-level AI legislation
- [BSA US AI policy tracker](https://www.bsa.org/policy-filings) — industry-curated state tracker

### Other jurisdictions
- [UK AI Safety Institute](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/) — frontier safety evaluations
- [UK DSIT — AI Regulation](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/department-for-science-innovation-and-technology) — UK pro-innovation framework
- [Canada — AIDA](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/innovation-better-canada/en/artificial-intelligence-and-data-act) — Artificial Intelligence and Data Act
- [Singapore — IMDA AI Verify](https://aiverifyfoundation.sg/) — AI testing framework
- [Japan — AI Strategy Council](https://www8.cao.go.jp/cstp/ai/) — METI/government AI policy
- [OECD AI Policy Observatory](https://oecd.ai/) — cross-jurisdictional tracker and dashboard

## Adding new sources

When adding a source, include:
- A short descriptor (what makes it useful, what it covers)
- The URL
- The category that fits best (add a new category if none fit)

Prefer primary sources (regulators, official documents) over secondary commentary when both are available.
